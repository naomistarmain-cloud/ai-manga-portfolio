# CUT Design

## Mandatory rules

- Treat one CUT as one primary action.
- Do not increase CUT count merely to lengthen the work.
- Do not divide a simple action without a clear narrative or camera reason.
- Do not change approved dialogue, plot, character settings, or ending.
- Keep dialogue short and avoid splitting CUTs only to accommodate dialogue.
- Choose one type for each CUT: video, still, still plus zoom, or still plus pan.
- State the start condition and end condition clearly.
- Use a duration no longer than the action requires.
- Avoid hand contact and complex interactions unless essential.

## CUT output format

For every CUT provide:

- CUT number
- Type
- Estimated duration
- Narrative purpose
- Composition and subject placement
- Start condition
- One primary action
- End condition
- Camera direction
- Dialogue or narration
- Sound effect notes
- Start-image requirement
- End-image requirement
- Continuity notes
- Generation risk

## Review

After drafting, check:

1. No duplicated or missing CUT numbers.
2. Every CUT advances story, emotion, or necessary information.
3. No CUT contains multiple competing actions.
4. Total duration remains within 180 seconds.
5. The sequence reads clearly in vertical 9:16.
