# Vertical Short Anime Story Design

## Goal

Create emotionally clear, visually strong short anime stories that can be produced with current AI image and video tools.

## Duration

- Standard target: 60–90 seconds.
- Extended range: 90–180 seconds only when the story requires it.
- Absolute maximum: 180 seconds.
- Prefer the shortest duration that communicates the story clearly.
- Never pad a story to approach the maximum duration.

## Format

- Primary aspect ratio: vertical 9:16.
- Keep the main subject readable on a smartphone screen.
- Avoid important action spread across the far left and right edges.
- Use depth, foreground, background, vertical movement, close-ups, tilts, and reveals to create scale.
- Leave reasonable top and bottom safe space for platform UI and captions.

## Story principles

1. Open with a strong image, action, mystery, or emotional hook.
2. Establish the situation quickly.
3. Avoid lengthy exposition.
4. Show information through action, props, environments, reactions, framing, and short dialogue.
5. Maintain a clear emotional or narrative progression.
6. Include a discovery, reversal, decision, escalation, or emotional change.
7. End with a memorable image, revelation, question, or emotional aftertaste.

## AI production principles

- Use one primary action per CUT.
- Avoid complex simultaneous physical actions.
- Avoid unnecessary hand contact between characters.
- Avoid crowd scenes unless essential.
- Avoid frequent costume, location, or lighting changes.
- Keep character positions, directions, props, and environments consistent.
- Use start and end images when they improve generation stability.

## Output

Produce:

1. Logline
2. Theme and emotional goal
3. Estimated duration
4. Beginning, development, turn, and ending
5. Scene outline
6. AI-generation risks
7. Items requiring approval before CUT design
